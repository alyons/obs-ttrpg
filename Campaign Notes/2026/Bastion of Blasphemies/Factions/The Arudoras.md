The Arudoras were an influential noble family who dwelt in a castle in eastern Varno. They and their estate vanished one awful night just over 500 years ago, and ever since, their family castle has been known as Bastardhall. The Arudora family crest is a serpentine creature with a long neck, a long tail, and four flippers curled around a sword in front of a stylized image of the castle.

The crest is a castle in the background (Bastard Hall) with a lake monster encircling a sword on top of a shield.
