# Bastion of Blasphemies
Castle Arudora was cut off from the rest of the world by some dreadful curse. Once every 100 years, the castle would seemingly rebuild itself and a carriage would go out to kidnap people. We were originally hired by Florin to investigate the island after it kidnapped her parents in the last event to have occurred.
## Castle Exterior
The land around the castle is not particularly wide, approximately within 100 (?) feet of the shore line. Areas around the castle were plague by monsters and haunts of various types, though through our exploration we have found and resolved some of them.
The lake surrounding the castle is stalked by a lake monster that destroyed our carriage and kills or maims anything that gets in the water. We have realized that even getting close to the water can 'summon' the creature to us, meaning that fishing or sailing are out of the question.
### Fishing Village
At the northwestern corner of the island is a small fishing village. It was originally inhabited by gridylows, who it seems were some of the villagers turned into monsters when the curse took the island.
As we have rescued various people from being trapped as spirits on the island, their souls have returned to the village and some buildings have been restored.
Aron runs the tavern and while he can't provide us with any food, it might serve as a reasonable place to rest for the moment.
Esmira ran the school and offered to help us research the history of the island and consolidate the knowledge we gather while exploring the castle.
Banijer runs the Trading Post and we might be able to buy limited supplies from him. He also ran a farm on the opposite side of the island, though that is where he was transformed into a monster and he killed his family.
Ilana runs the workshop next to the trading post and offered to craft us various implements.
### Northern Gate
There is a gate on the north side of the island seemingly leading into the castle. That is where we met Lucenaro. Originally disguised as a man, he is a monitor for Pharasma. He wants to help cleans this place of it's curse as it would help the souls go to rest as well as him being trapped here. He let us know that the whole area seems to be adrift in the ethereal plane.
### The Summoning Stones
To the northeast of the island sits a small isle with a circle of stones on it. Those stones seem to form a gate, though they seem to be the destination portion of the gate. When we cleaned off the stones, we found the spirit of Esmira, the former headmistress of the school in the fishing village.
###  Castle Drain
On the eastern wall, we found what seemed to be a drainage system which we could theoretically use to enter the castle. Given that it is part of the water system, we decided against using it at the moment.
### Farmlands
On the eastern side of the island there are a few plots of farmland with ruined houses. A scarecrow sits in the middle of the fields.
Upon our second visit to the farmlands, we inspected the scarecrow triggering it to attack us. Once we vanquished the monster, it turned into Banijer and was teleported back to the village.
